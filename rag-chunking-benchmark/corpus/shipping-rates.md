# Shipping rates and delivery times

Kestrel Outfitters ships with four carriers. The tables below give the delivery time and price for each service, by carrier and destination region. Delivery times count from dispatch, not from the order date. Prices are per parcel up to 20 kg; bulky items are quoted at checkout.
